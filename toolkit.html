The Toolkit — free, open-access, ready to adopt

Enabling U.S. nonprofits to operationalize their duty of care.

Implementation Toolkit v1.0 · August 2026 · CC BY 4.0

97% of U.S. charitable nonprofits operate on budgets under $5 million (National Council of Nonprofits, 2019), a scale at which dedicated safeguarding staff is rarely possible. All tools are free under CC BY 4.0 — adopt them, adapt them to your context, and share them.

New: score the self-assessment in your browser. All 30 indicators, with live domain subtotals and your score band. Nothing is submitted or stored. Open the interactive self-assessment →

1

Organizational Safeguarding Policy Template

A board-ready policy a small nonprofit can adapt without outside help: a code of conduct adapting all six IASC Core Principles, narrowed to protect program participants while leaving staff-on-staff conduct to ordinary employment law; sections on screening, training, reporting channels, response, partner flow-down, and board accountability; and an appendix mapping every section to published standards, including a definitional-basis note anchoring key terms to UN and U.S. law. Sections 1–3 are the minimum viable policy for a very small, all-volunteer organization; Sections 4–9 complete the whole-of-organization system.

→ Download the policy template (v1.2, Word)

2

Safeguarding Self-Assessment

Thirty indicators across six domains, keyed to published standards, with scoring bands that turn a raw count into a starting point. No registration, no data collection — score it internally and keep the results.

→ Complete it in your browser · download from Zenodo: PDF · Word · citation

3

Adopter Feedback Form

Fifteen to twenty minutes that shape the next published version: what your organization used, what it adopted, what needed adaptation, and what was wrong for your state, size, or population. Complete it in your own copy and email it back — the form can be returned anonymously.

→ Download from Zenodo: PDF · Word (easier to complete on screen) · citation

4

Safeguarding Training Outlines

Three sessions your own staff deliver: a 60-minute orientation, a 45-minute annual refresher, and a 90-minute responder session. As an iterative resource, this curriculum is actively expanded and improved using the real-world feedback returned by our early adopters.

→ Status: Forthcoming. Scheduled for release following the first round of adopter feedback.

How to Adopt (Self-Directed Pathway)

  1. Benchmark your baseline with Tool 2: Complete the 30-indicator self-assessment to identify priority risk areas and determine your organization’s immediate starting point.
  2. Adopt the core policy using Tool 1: Review and ratify Sections 1–3 at a single board meeting as a minimum viable policy, or implement Sections 1–9 for whole-of-organization coverage.
  3. Deliver internal staff training with Tool 4: Facilitate staff orientation and responder sessions internally on your own schedule using the modular, fully scripted outlines.
  4. Return structured field feedback via Tool 3: Submit observations on what worked, adaptations required for your operational context, or state-specific nuances. Adopter feedback directly drives ongoing sector research and upcoming framework revisions.

All materials are free. The project does not sell tools, training, or consulting; feedback from adopters informs published, sector-level research.
