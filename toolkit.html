The Toolkit — free, open-access, ready to adopt

Free tools U.S. nonprofits can adapt to protect the people they serve.

New to this? Start with two pages.

The Safeguarding Quick Start lists the six basic safeguards, what each looks like in practice, and where to find the template for it — plus six questions to see where you stand.

Download the Quick Start (PDF) Send feedback — info@pseacrosswalk.org

97% of U.S. charitable nonprofits operate on budgets under $5 million (National Council of Nonprofits, 2019), a scale at which dedicated safeguarding staff is rarely possible. All tools are free under CC BY 4.0 — adopt them, adapt them to your context, and share them.

These are operational resources, not legal advice. They do not certify compliance with any law, funder condition, or accreditation standard. Have your completed policy reviewed by counsel licensed in your state before adoption. The author is not admitted to practice law in the United States.

Tool 1

Organizational Safeguarding Policy Template

A board-ready policy a small nonprofit can adapt without outside help: a code of conduct adapting all six IASC Core Principles, narrowed to protect program participants while leaving staff-on-staff conduct to ordinary employment law; sections on screening, training, reporting channels, response, partner flow-down, and board accountability; and an appendix mapping every section to published standards, including a definitional-basis note anchoring key terms to UN and U.S. law. Sections 1–3 and 6 are the starter policy for a small or all-volunteer organization — a foundation, not a complete system; Sections 4–9 complete the whole-of-organization system.

Tool 2

Safeguarding Self-Assessment

Thirty-two indicators across six domains, keyed to published standards, with scoring bands that turn a raw count into a starting point. No registration, no data collection — score it internally and keep the results.

Tool 3

Adopter Feedback Form

Fifteen to twenty minutes that shape the next published version: what your organization used, what it adopted, what needed adaptation, and what was wrong for your state, size, or population. Complete it in your own copy and email it back — the form can be returned anonymously.

Tool 4

Safeguarding Training Outlines

Three sessions your own staff deliver: a 60-minute orientation, a 45-minute annual refresher, and a 90-minute responder session — plus a 3-minute intake script, used with every new participant when the Participant Charter is handed over. Responses to the Adopter Feedback Form (Tool 3) shape the next version of each tool.
