Safeguarding research for the U.S. nonprofit sector

Free, open-access frameworks and tools that help nonprofits protect the people they serve.

The United States has roughly 1.9 million nonprofit organizations and no general safeguarding baseline that reaches them. I research that gap — and publish practical, open-access tools to help close it.

    A thirteen-element crosswalk of international safeguarding standards against U.S. law, and a comparative study of why implementation fails.
