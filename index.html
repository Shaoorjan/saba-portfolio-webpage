Bridging the Safeguarding Gap in the U.S. Nonprofit Sector

Open-access frameworks equipping U.S. nonprofits to operationalize their duty of care.

With over 1.9 million nonprofit organizations in the U.S., the sector critically lacks a unified safeguarding baseline. My work addresses this systemic vulnerability by translating international PSEA mandates into practical, open-access frameworks that organizations can implement immediately.
